# The Veil brand assets

The cocktail and curtain photos, eye/keyhole symbol, and keyhole photo mask
come from the venue's supplied brand guidelines. The SVG geometry is extracted
from the original artwork; it is not a recreated logo.

Geist Regular, Medium, and SemiBold are self-hosted Google Fonts files, including
Cyrillic glyphs. Their SIL Open Font License is included in `OFL.txt`.

BRON remains the software identity in the header and footer. The venue name,
theme, imagery, and favicon are configured separately for `secret-veil`.

`floor-plans/vip.png` is an unchanged copy of the venue-supplied VIP illustration
in the ignored `context/venue/` directory. Its markers retain the original SVG
table IDs 28 and 29. Capacities and bookable VIP room boundaries remain unknown.

The active hall uses a code-rendered diagram of the latest handwritten seating
chart: small round tables 12-23, larger rectangular tables 1-11 and 24-25, bar,
hookah area, and restrooms. The shaded entrance vestibule is at the lower left;
the shaded upper-right room is the staircase to the VIP rooms on the second
floor. VIP interiors remain in their separate tab. Source photographs are not
published. Old hall, balcony, and terrace PNGs are no longer shipped; outdoor
areas are omitted while out of season.

The hall uses native SVG furniture and architectural details in the VIP plan's
muted palette, with wider aisles and separate transparent selection targets.
Continuous upholstery bands are illustrative and do not assign seat counts.
